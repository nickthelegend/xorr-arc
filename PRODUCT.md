# XORR on Arc

A self-custodial trading desk for a person who wants a small council to trade within an explicit on-chain permission.
The primary workflow is fund USDC, grant a cap/venue/expiry, convene the rule-based council, inspect the receipt, revoke.
Arc testnet is the live verified environment. Mainnet integrations are fork evidence only; unsupported external flows
are labelled Not run. The council is deterministic, not an LLM. Testnet liquidity is XORR-seeded.

The frontend is Expo for web and mobile. This port uses the finished xorr-metropolis visual system as its authority.
No new brand direction is requested. Success means the same wallet-and-agent experience with Arc facts and real API data.
